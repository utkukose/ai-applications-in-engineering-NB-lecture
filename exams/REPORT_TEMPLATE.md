# Report template

Reports for the weekly research assignments and for the midterm and final projects follow this structure. Write in English, in the third person, with short paragraphs and references in square brackets, for example [1] or [2, 3]. Include the executed notebook as a separate file.

## Title

A specific title that names the problem and the method, followed by the author's name, department and student number, and the course.

## Abstract

About 150 words: the problem, the approach, the main quantitative result and its meaning.

## 1. Introduction

The engineering problem, why it matters, and the question the report answers.

## 2. Related work

What published studies and standards say about the problem, with at least six scholarly or official sources.

## 3. Data and methods

Data sources with licences and limits, preprocessing, the models or algorithms, their settings, and the evaluation design, including baselines and splits.

## 4. Results

Tables and figures with captions and units. Results over several random seeds where the methods are stochastic.

## 5. Discussion

Interpretation, limitations, threats to validity, and what would be needed before the result could support a real decision. For the final project, a short risk assessment with the EU AI Act categories and the NIST AI RMF functions.

## 6. Conclusion

Three to five sentences.

## Statement on tools

Which software and which generative AI tools were used, for what, and how their output was verified.

## References

A numbered list of all cited works in a consistent style.
