# Disciplines: Where every department meets the course

The course serves the nineteen departments of the Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences at Süleyman Demirel University and engineering students everywhere. Every week contains a table of discipline challenges; the map below collects them by department. Running examples of the core notebooks are chosen so that each department appears several times: concrete and soil for civil engineering, bearings, pumps and machine failures for mechanical engineering, power plants and motor control for electrical engineering, beans and rice for food engineering, well logs and earthquakes for the Earth sciences, and so on.
