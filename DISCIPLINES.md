# Disciplines: Where every department meets the course

The course serves the nineteen departments of the Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences at Süleyman Demirel University and engineering students everywhere. The discipline challenges of each week are part of its overview page; the map below shows, for every department, the weeks with a dedicated challenge and links to them. Running examples of the core notebooks are chosen so that each department appears several times: concrete and soil for civil engineering, bearings, pumps and machine failures for mechanical engineering, power plants and motor control for electrical engineering, beans and rice for food engineering, well logs and earthquakes for the Earth sciences, and so on.

## Fields beyond the faculty

| Field | Where to start |
|---|---|
| Aerospace engineering | Airfoil self-noise regression (Week 7), gas turbine emissions (Weeks 7 and 10), PID tuning (Week 6), remaining useful life of turbofan engines (Week 12) |
| Biomedical engineering | Signal features and anomaly detection (Week 9), image classification and Grad-CAM (Week 11), responsible AI and regulation (Week 14) |
| Petroleum and natural gas engineering | Well-log facies clustering (Week 9), physics-informed learning for flow problems (Week 14) |
| Materials and metallurgical engineering | Superconductor critical temperature (Weeks 7 and 10), steel plate faults (Week 8), steel surface images (Week 11) |
| Marine and naval engineering | Path planning with constraints (Week 2), swarm and evolutionary design (Weeks 5 and 6), forecasting (Week 12) |
| Agricultural and biosystems engineering | Dry bean and rice classification (Week 8), crop images with transfer learning (Week 11) |
| Energy systems engineering | Power plant output (Weeks 7 and 10), building heating loads (Week 7), demand forecasting (Week 12), learned thermostats and digital twins (Week 14) |
| Mechatronics and robotics | Agents (Week 1), search and hybrid A* (Week 2), fuzzy and PID control (Weeks 4 and 6), reinforcement learning (Week 14) |
| Software engineering | Rule engines (Week 3), retrieval and code-generating language models (Week 13) |
| Transportation engineering | Route planning (Week 2), traffic sign recognition (Week 11), forecasting (Week 12) |
