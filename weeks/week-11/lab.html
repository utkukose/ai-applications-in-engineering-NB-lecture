Convolution explorer: Filters, feature maps and receptive fields

Prof. Dr. Utku Kose, Süleyman Demirel University

Course homeWeek overviewLecture pageColab notebookPDF notes

Part A lets a greyscale image be drawn or generated, applies a chosen or hand-edited 3 by 3 filter, and shows the feature map, its ReLU and a 2 by 2 max pooling, which are the three steps of a convolutional block [1]. Part B computes output sizes, parameter counts and receptive fields for a stack of convolution and pooling layers.
